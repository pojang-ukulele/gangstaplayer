    GANGSTA PLAYER PRO 3 - MANUAL

    KEYS & CONTROLS

    • SPACE : Play / Pause Main Deck
    • ↑ / ↓ : Navigate Playlist
    • SHIFT + ↑/↓ : Multi-Select Tracks
    • ← / → : Skip 5 Seconds Back / Forward
    • ENTER : Universal Stop / Emergency Reset
    • DELETE : Remove Track(s) from Playlist
    • PAD KEYS : Trigger Pad (Hold to Play)
    • CTRL + PAD KEY : Hold to Record (Input Audio)

    FEATURES

    • LOAD SAMPLE: Right-click any pad and select "LOAD SAMPLE" to load audio. You can also drag & drop files onto pads.
    • RECORD PAD: Click the small [REC] button on a pad or use CTRL+Key shortcut. Records your mic/input to that pad.
    • MASTER REC: The big [REC] button records the ENTIRE OUTPUT (mix) to a high-quality WAV file.
    • SYNC: Right-click a loaded pad and select "SYNC" to match its tempo to the Main Deck. A ✓ checkmark shows when SYNC is active.
    • CHOPPER & SCREWER: The slider controls global playback speed. Slow it down for that screw effect. Magnetic snap at 100%.
    • AI AUTO-MIX: Seamlessly mixes between tracks with pro DJ effects. Toggle options in Settings. Use Next/Prev buttons for fast mixes. Customize fade lengths in Settings!
    • SETTINGS: App automatically saves your preferences. Use "RESTORE DEFAULTS" to reset.
